Frequently Asked Questions

What is Patient Visit Average (PVA) in a chiropractic practice?

Patient Visit Average (PVA) is the average number of visits a patient completes during their care plan. It is one of the most powerful revenue levers in a chiropractic practice — a small increase in PVA can generate tens of thousands of dollars in additional annual revenue without acquiring a single new patient.

How much revenue can increasing PVA add to my practice?

A practice with 10 new patients monthly that increases its PVA by just five visits at a $50 average collection adds $30,000 in annual revenue — without acquiring any additional patients.

What are the most effective ways to increase PVA?

The most effective strategies include automated appointment reminders, frictionless 24/7 rescheduling, same-day follow-up for patients without a next appointment, family block scheduling, and incentivizing patients to map out their full care plan upfront.
