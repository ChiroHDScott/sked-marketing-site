How to Craft Drip Campaigns that Educate Patients at Exactly the Right Time

How to build milestone-driven drip campaigns that keep patients engaged and improve retention — without time-consuming manual follow-up.

Want to keep building trust and educating patients on chiropractic care long after their first visit? Or educate pregnant patients about how chiropractic treatment benefits them at each stage of their pregnancy?

Drip campaigns are the perfect tool. But how do you craft drip campaigns that are both engaging and educational… without taking focus away from patient care?

Join us Thursday, June 25 at 12:30pm Eastern to learn the easy way to build drip campaigns that educate patients at exactly the right time.

Erik Kowalke, DC & Becks Drobnik will show you how to:

  • Build milestone-driven drip campaigns that deliver the right message at exactly the right moment
  • Keep educating and building trust with patients long after their first visit
  • Create stage-specific campaigns — like guiding pregnant patients through each stage of their pregnancy
  • Improve retention and engagement without time-consuming manual follow-up
