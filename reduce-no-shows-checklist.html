Frequently Asked Questions

Why do chiropractic patients miss appointments?

Patients most commonly miss appointments due to barriers like phone-only scheduling, lack of automated reminders, and losing momentum between visits. When life gets busy and there is no digital reinforcement, appointments fall off the mental radar.

How much revenue does a chiropractic practice lose to no-shows?

If a clinic loses just 10 patients per month before completing a 12-visit care plan at $60 per visit, that represents over $7,200 in lost monthly revenue — not counting the marketing spend already invested to acquire those patients.

What is the most effective way to reduce no-shows at a chiropractic clinic?

Automated text message reminders, easy self-scheduling options, and proactive reactivation campaigns are consistently the most effective strategies for reducing no-shows and keeping schedules full.
